# Public comparison copies

The files in original-sanitized are copies of the original CS 340 artifact.
Historical username/password literals have been replaced with explicit redaction markers.
Notebook outputs, execution counts, and notebook/cell metadata have been removed.
They are provided for source comparison, not as a runnable or retested original application.
Untouched originals and their original checksum manifest remain in the private course submission package.
PUBLIC_SHA256.txt records hashes of these public copies, not the untouched originals.
